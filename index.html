van Veen lab research at UCLA.

We study women’s health across biological scales—from hormone signaling within individual cells to neural circuits, physiology, and disease. Our research brings together systems and cell biology, neuroscience, and endocrinology, allowing the questions to determine the methods and direction of our work. We also develop new tools, such as neuro-seeER, to make hormone responses visible and enable questions that could not previously be addressed.
