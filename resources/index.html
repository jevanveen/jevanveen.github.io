New paper · August 28, 2026

A bifunctional optical reporter for tracking estrogen response dynamics in neurons

Cara, Guthman, Mishra, Hall, Yokoyama, Sandoval, Gregg, Aghi, Soma, Correa, Falkner, and van Veen

neuro-seeER distinguishes estrogen-sensitive neurons from neurons actively responding to estrogen. It can be used with standard microscopy and photometry approaches in vitro and in vivo, including longitudinal measurements in behaving animals.
